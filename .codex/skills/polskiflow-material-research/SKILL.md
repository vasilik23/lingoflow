# LingoFlow material research

1. Read `docs/content-sources.md` and the target topic in `docs/content-roadmap.md`.
2. Browse primary sources. Do not treat search snippets, LLM output, or free access as evidence of reuse rights.
3. Prefer original LingoFlow content. For external material, verify the exact item, author, license, URL, retrieval date, attribution, and permitted adaptations.
4. Reject unclear licenses and `NC`/`ND` material unless the maintainer explicitly approves it.
5. Cross-check language facts with authoritative dictionaries or official linguistic sources; distinguish inference from sourced fact.
6. Return a concise research packet: learning objective, verified facts, candidate material, license decision, attribution, and risks.
7. Add approved sources to the registry before content publication.

Never copy commercial textbooks, app exercises, contemporary articles, or audio without explicit compatible rights.
